USB-C SUPERSPEED HUB - COMPONENT SELECTION REVIEW
============================================================
Date: 2 November 2026
Baseline: USB-C-SS-Hub-Case-C-BOM-Hand-Solder.xlsx

Scope: suitability of the selected parts, component combinations,
ratings, packages and values. This report replaces the previous broad
error report for the current discussion. Unfinished schematic/PCB work
and downloaded CAD-library problems are excluded.

CONFIRMED = a specification or BOM-description conflict.
LIKELY = a mismatch inferred from component specifications; needs validation.
REVIEW = a selection trade-off or unresolved requirement, not a proven fault.
UNVERIFIED MPN = the exact orderable part number was not established.

MAIN CONCLUSION
---------------
The hub/controller/regulator concept is plausible, but several choices
should be corrected or qualified before buying components. Highest priorities:
switch-enable compatibility, current capability, TVS suitability, crystal
loading, exact inductor identification and connector mechanical compatibility.

01. TPS2553DBVR WITH HD3SS3220: ENABLE-POLARITY MISMATCH
Status: CONFIRMED for the BOM's proposed direct ID-to-EN use.
BOM: U3-U8; rows 9-14, especially notes L9-L11.

HD3SS3220 ID asserts low on downstream attachment. TPS2553 enables high.
These parts are not directly compatible in the connection described by
the BOM. This does not make TPS2553 an intrinsically unsuitable switch.

Recommendation:
- Keep TPS2553 only if deliberate inversion/control logic is included.
- TPS2552DBVR is an active-low alternative worth considering when an
  active-low enable is required. Do not treat that substitution as a
  complete solution to hub power control or Type-C discharge requirements.
- Choose enable polarity, reverse-current behavior and output discharge
  together. TPS2553 does not provide integrated quick output discharge.
Sources: [1], [2].

02. ~1 A SWITCH SELECTION DOES NOT SUPPORT 3 A ADVERTISEMENT
Status: CONFIRMED mismatch between intended port capability and advertisement.
BOM: U3-U5 CURRENT_MODE notes; U6-U8 and Rilim.

The proposed CURRENT_MODE high setting advertises 3 A. TPS2553 with
24.9 kohm ILIM is approximately a 1 A solution, not a 3 A solution.
The family is specified for up to 1.5 A continuous load under applicable
conditions; changing the ILIM resistor cannot turn it into a 3 A switch.

With 24.9 kohm, the datasheet equations give approximately:
  Nominal limit:              1.036 A
  Minimum including 1% R:     0.953 A
  Maximum including 1% R:     1.130 A

Three 1 A outputs alone require 15 W at 5 V, before the hub's own losses.
Recommendation: retain the ~1 A switch only for a suitably restricted port
policy. If 3 A per port is a real requirement, reselect the switches AND
the power source/budget. A larger connector current rating does not solve it.
Sources: [1], [2].

03. PESD5V0S1UL IS NOT A SUB-6 V INPUT CLAMP
Status: CONFIRMED protection-selection and package-description problem.
BOM: Dtv, row 36; buck note L19.

The '5 V' designation does not mean the device clamps transients at 5 V.
Its breakdown/clamping characteristics do not establish protection below
the regulator's 6 V absolute maximum. It is an ESD protection diode, not
a precision overvoltage limiter.

PESD5V0S1UL package: SOD882 / DFN1006-2, not the listed SOD323.
TLV62569 input: 5.5 V recommended maximum; 6 V absolute maximum.

Recommendation: correct the package and select an input-protection scheme
using actual clamping voltage at the relevant surge current, source
impedance and regulator limits. Consider an overvoltage-disconnecting
device if the requirement includes sustained overvoltage. Do not simply
substitute another diode labelled '5 V'.
Sources: [4], [7].

04. TPD2EUSB30DRTR: QUANTITY AND PACKAGE DESCRIPTION
Status: CONFIRMED for full connector-side SuperSpeed protection.
BOM: Uesd_ss, row 34; quantity 8.

This is a two-signal-channel device in the DRT three-pin package,
not the listed 'SOT-5'. Each Type-C receptacle exposes four SuperSpeed
differential pairs, or eight signal conductors, before its orientation mux.
Four receptacles therefore need 32 protected conductors. Eight devices
cover only 16 conductors.

Recommendation: use 16 of these two-channel devices for all connector-side
SS lanes, or select an appropriate array combination with equivalent
coverage. The part itself supports USB 3.0; it is not rejected solely on
data rate. Recheck channel loading and clamping for any replacement.
Source: [6].

05. ABM8-24.000MHZ-B2-T WITH TWO 12 pF LOAD CAPACITORS
Status: LIKELY load-capacitor mismatch; crystal itself is plausible.
BOM: Y1 and C_y, rows 6-7.

The selected ABM8 variant has the standard 18 pF load specification.
Two equal 12 pF external capacitors contribute 6 pF in series:

  CL approximately = C1*C2/(C1+C2) + effective stray capacitance
                   = 6 pF + effective stray capacitance.

Reaching 18 pF would require about 12 pF of effective stray capacitance.
That must be established, not assumed. Incorrect loading shifts frequency
and can affect oscillator startup margin.

Recommendation: keep the crystal if its complete frequency budget is
suitable, but recalculate C_y. As an illustrative calculation only, 2-5 pF
effective stray capacitance would imply equal capacitors of about 32-26 pF.
Do not freeze a replacement value without accounting for IC/board loading.
The '2' option specifies initial +/-20 ppm tolerance; it is not the full
temperature, aging and load-error budget.
Sources: [3], [8].

06. XFL4015-102MEB: EXACT INDUCTOR PART NUMBER NOT VERIFIED
Status: UNVERIFIED MPN / likely catalog-number error.
BOM: L32, row 20.

Coilcraft's current XFL4015 table lists 0.18, 0.33, 0.47, 0.70 and 1.2 uH
values. It does not list the BOM's XFL4015-102 1.0 uH option. An exact-part
search did not establish an official listing. This is not proof that a
custom or historical part never existed, but the BOM entry is not verified.

Recommendation: obtain an official datasheet/orderable number before
purchase. Coilcraft XFL4020-102 is a documented 1.0 uH candidate; verify
its full suffix, dimensions, height and current ratings before selecting it.
Do not silently change 4015 to 4020 and assume identical mechanics.

The numerical choice of 1 uH is not automatically an error. At nominal
5 V input, 1.1 V output, 1.5 MHz and 0.7 A load:
  Ideal inductor ripple approximately 0.572 A peak-to-peak.
  Ideal peak current approximately 0.986 A.
Final selection must also cover tolerance, startup, faults, heating and
the regulator's supported inductor/output-capacitor combinations.
Sources: [4], [9], [10].

07. TLV75533PDBVR: THERMAL MARGIN AT 200 mA
Status: REVIEW; valid voltage/current selection, conditional thermal suitability.
BOM: U31, row 16, load assumption <=0.20 A.

At 5 V input and 3.3 V / 0.2 A output, dissipation is approximately 0.34 W.
TI gives DBV junction-to-ambient figures of 100.8 C/W on its EVM and
231.1 C/W under the stated JEDEC test conditions. Applied illustratively,
these give roughly 34 C and 79 C temperature rises. Neither is a prediction
for this project's thermal environment.

Recommendation: retain DBV only after checking maximum input, actual load,
ambient and copper-dependent thermal performance. The 500 mA headline is
not a promise of 500 mA at every input/output voltage and ambient.
The DYD variant has improved thermal performance but an exposed thermal
pad, adding an assembly requirement. It is not an interchangeable
hand-soldering assumption merely because both packages are called SOT-23-5.
Source: [5].

08. 124019572112A: CONNECTOR / BOARD-THICKNESS COMPATIBILITY
Status: CONFIRMED catalog/BOM conflict; confirm against the controlled drawing.
BOM: J1-J4, rows 30-33; board specification row 44.

Amphenol identifies this part as a mid-mount, dual-row SMT Type-C connector.
Its product page lists a PCB thickness range of 0.80-1.20 mm, while the BOM
specifies a 1.6 mm board. This is a mechanical component-selection issue,
independent of whether routing has been completed.

Recommendation: verify the exact mechanical drawing. Select a connector
explicitly compatible with the intended 1.6 mm board, or deliberately
change the board thickness. For manual assembly, inspect access to both
contact rows; through-hole shell tabs do not make every signal pin accessible.
Do not substitute a USB2-only Type-C connector for this SuperSpeed application.
Source: [11].

09. BLM18PG300SN1D: CURRENT AND DC-RESISTANCE MARGIN
Status: REVIEW; BOM resistance assumption is not the worst-case rating.
BOM: FB32, row 25.

Murata lists BLM18PG300SN1 with 1 A rated current and 0.05 ohm maximum
DC resistance. The BOM uses approximately 0.04 ohm and asks for <0.05 ohm.
At 0.7 A, 0.05 ohm causes a 35 mV drop. The nominal 1.096 V regulator
setting would then be approximately 1.061 V after the bead, before other
tolerances and transient effects. This alone is not a demonstrated failure.

Recommendation: check maximum rail current and voltage margin using
maximum DCR, not only a typical value. A lower-DCR/higher-current bead can
improve margin, but its impedance and resonance with capacitors must also
be suitable. Do not choose a replacement only by its 100 MHz impedance.
Source: [12].

10. QFN, USON, 0402 AND MID-MOUNT PARTS: ASSEMBLY FIT
Status: CONFIRMED limitation if 'hand solder' means iron-only assembly.
BOM: TUSB8041A, HD3SS3220, ESD arrays, small passives and connectors.

Changing the two regulators to SOT-23 does not make the whole BOM equally
easy to assemble. The hub/mux packages include inaccessible underside pads;
some protection devices and passives are very small.

Recommendation: retain these ICs if stencil/reflow or suitable hot-air
assembly is available. Otherwise consider preassembled modules or outsourced
assembly. Larger 0603/0805 passives may help where electrically appropriate,
but they are not a blanket replacement rule for every high-speed node.
Sources: [1], [3], [6], [11].

11. TLV62569PDDCR: KEEP THE PG VARIANT; VERIFY FILTER COMBINATION
Status: CONDITIONALLY SUITABLE, not a confirmed bad regulator choice.
BOM: U32, row 19; L32, C32o, R32t/R32b.

The selected PDDC device is a six-pin SOT-23 buck with power-good output.
It can support the intended nominal 1.1 V supply. A non-PG DBV variant
would not preserve the specified reset-control function.

124 kohm / 150 kohm sets approximately 1.096 V using the 0.6 V reference.
The 150 kohm bottom resistor is within the datasheet's 200 kohm guideline.
TI specifies validated L/C combinations; check the chosen effective output
capacitance with 1 uH at 1.1 V rather than selecting L from frequency alone.

Recommendation: keep the selected buck provisionally; qualify its complete
filter and transient performance. Correct the BOM's '5.5 V absolute max'
note: 5.5 V is the operating input limit; absolute maximum is 6 V.
Source: [4].

12. HIGH-CAPACITANCE MLCCS AND 6.3 V INPUT CAPACITORS
Status: REVIEW / exact manufacturer records not verified for every MPN.
BOM: C31i, C31o, C32i, C32o, C32h, C10u.

Do not assume the marked capacitance is available under DC bias. The
0402 10 uF and 0603 high-capacitance selections particularly warrant review.
For 5 V input capacitors, a 6.3 V rating also gives limited transient margin.
Neither fact proves a capacitor is wrong by itself.

Recommendation: obtain the exact Murata part record and bias curves for
each selected MPN, including GRM155R70J106ME15D and GRM188R70J226ME47D,
whose exact manufacturer listings were not established in this review.
Verify minimum effective capacitance, dielectric, dimensions and ratings.
Consider larger-case or higher-voltage parts when the effective capacitance
or voltage margin is inadequate. Do not approve a guessed MPN based on
its apparent encoding.

13. 180 uF INPUT BULK AND CSD17313Q2 REVERSE-BLOCKING ROLE
Status: REVIEW of component roles, not proof either individual part is faulty.
BOM: Cbulk row 41, Q1 row 29, input VBUS capacitance row 40.

A 180 uF capacitor is not automatically wrong, but it creates substantial
input charging demand. Its selection needs a compatible inrush-limited
power path. Likewise, a single N-channel MOSFET described as 'gate from
VBUS detect' is not, on its own, a validated high-side reverse-blocking choice.

Recommendation: select input capacitance together with the input switch or
eFuse. If retaining Q1, validate gate-drive headroom, body-diode orientation
and reverse-blocking operating states. Otherwise select an integrated
solution with the required inrush and reverse-current functions.

WHAT I WOULD KEEP PROVISIONALLY
------------------------------
- TUSB8041ARGCR as the 5 Gbps hub, provided its package is assemblable.
- HD3SS3220 as the CC/orientation-mux function, with a compatible port policy.
- TLV62569PDDCR as the core buck, after filter/thermal checks.
- TLV75533PDBVR only if the thermal estimate supports the actual conditions.
- ABM8-24.000MHZ-B2-T, with corrected/validated load capacitors.
- TPD2EUSB30DRTR as a candidate ESD device, with corrected coverage/package.

These are provisional selections, not a blanket approval of every BOM MPN.
No component replacement has been applied to the original workbook.
