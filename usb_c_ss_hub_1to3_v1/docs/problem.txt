CURRENT FINDINGS
========================================================================
01. Engineering notes were written into the DNP column
Status: HIGH / CONFIRMED
Cells: BOM!K4; K7,K9:K14,K20,K34,K36,K44

K4 is labelled DNP, but 11 cells contain engineering instructions. This makes
populate/do-not-populate interpretation ambiguous. An export that treats any nonblank
DNP cell as exclusion could incorrectly omit components.

Recommended correction/check: Move the engineering text to column L (Note), reconcile
existing notes, and reserve K for a defined DNP status. Do not mark these parts DNP
merely because K is nonblank.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

02. Conflicting downstream current instructions
Status: HIGH / CONFIRMED
Cells: BOM!K9,L9; Spec!B7; Changes!C5

K9 and Spec!B7 specify default current, but L9 still instructs CURRENT_MODE to 5 V for 3
A. The selected TPS2552/24.9 kohm combination remains approximately a 1 A solution.
Default USB current is not a universal 0.9 A entitlement: USB2/USB3 and configuration
conditions matter.

Recommended correction/check: Delete the obsolete 3 A instruction. State one intended
mode consistently and describe it as Default USB current, subject to the applicable USB
operating/configuration conditions. The active-low TPS2552 choice itself fixes the
previous enable-polarity mismatch. [1,2]

------------------------------------------------------------------------

03. ESD purchasing calculations have been replaced with constants
Status: HIGH / CONFIRMED
Cells: BOM!J34; Buy_List!C23,E23

J34 is literal 6.72, C23 is literal 16, and E23 is literal 6.72. They currently agree
with 16 x USD 0.42, but later quantity or price edits will not reliably propagate. Both
totals can even remain equal while both are stale.

Recommended correction/check: Restore BOM!J34 to =B34*I34, Buy_List!C23 to
=SUMIF(BOM!D:D,A23,BOM!B:B), and Buy_List!E23 to =C23*D23. Then recalculate and compare
quantities and costs independently. These are proposed fixes; the workbook was not
edited.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

04. Old converter notes and incorrect voltage limit remain
Status: MEDIUM / CONFIRMED
Cells: BOM!L19,L36; Changes!C12:D12

L19 calls 5.5 V the TLV62569 absolute maximum. Its recommended VIN maximum is 5.5 V;
absolute maximum is 6 V. L36 still refers to removed TPS62822 and contradicts the new
ESD-only explanation in K36. Changes claims these notes were aligned, but they were not.

Recommended correction/check: Correct L19, replace the obsolete L36 statement, and
update Changes to reflect what is actually corrected. An absolute maximum is not an
allowable continuous operating target. [3]

------------------------------------------------------------------------

05. 27 pF crystal-load explanation does not match its stated stray capacitance
Status: MEDIUM / CONFIRMED ARITHMETIC
Cells: BOM!D7,F7,K7

K7 says the two 27 pF capacitors provide 18 pF CL with approximately 3 pF stray. The
usual effective-load calculation gives 27/2 + 3 = 16.5 pF. Two 27 pF capacitors reach 18
pF when effective stray is about 4.5 pF. The selected crystal has 18 pF nominal load.

Recommended correction/check: Correct the explanation. If 3 pF is the actual effective
stray estimate, equal 30 pF capacitors give 18 pF by this approximation. Keep 27 pF only
if the actual pin/board loading and frequency validation support it. This is not proof
that 27 pF cannot work. [4]

------------------------------------------------------------------------

06. Standoff part does not establish the claimed screw bundle
Status: MEDIUM / CONFIRMED
Cells: BOM!F45,D45; Buy_List!A33

F45 says M3 standoff + screw. Harwin R30-1000402 is a 4 mm body, M3 female-female brass
spacer. The manufacturer identifies the spacer, not a screw kit.

Recommended correction/check: Describe the row as the spacer only. If screws are
intended in the procurement scope, specify their own part numbers, length and
quantities, or document a vendor kit that really includes them. [5]

------------------------------------------------------------------------

07. Optional Q1 has no explicit population policy
Status: MEDIUM / CONFIRMED
Cells: BOM!L29,K29; BOM!J5:J45; Buy_List formulas

Q1 is described as optional, K29 is blank, and its USD 0.45 is included in the computed
total. Existing cost and quantity formulas do not inspect the DNP column at all.

Recommended correction/check: Choose whether the baseline includes Q1. If DNP is meant
to affect procurement, implement that policy consistently in both BOM and Buy_List.
Until then USD 59.57 means all listed quantities included, including Q1.

08. Old switch name remains in ILIM description
Status: LOW / CONFIRMED
Cells: BOM!F15; Spec!B7

F15 still identifies the resistor as TPS2553 ILIM, while U6-U8 and Spec now use TPS2552.
The family shares the relevant limit-setting method, so this is a stale description
rather than evidence that 24.9 kohm is wrong.

Recommended correction/check: Update F15 to TPS2552 and retain the approximately 1 A
wording, with minimum/maximum tolerance considered. [2]

09. Inductor saturation current omits its test definition
Status: LOW / CONFIRMED QUALIFICATION NEEDED
Cells: BOM!F20

XFL4020-102MEC is a verified 1 uH part. The quoted approximately 5.4 A is the 30%
inductance-drop saturation criterion. Coilcraft also specifies 4.5 A at 10% drop and 5.1
A at 20% drop.

Recommended correction/check: State the drop criterion alongside Isat. This is a
documentation qualification, not a demonstrated current-capacity failure for the
intended 0.7 A load. [6]

Formula text is present, but saved cached results are absent for formula cells,

11. PESD5V0S1UL remains ESD protection, not guaranteed low-voltage OVP
Status: HIGH / CONDITIONAL SELECTION RISK
Cells: BOM!D36,F36,K36; U31/U32 input ratings

The package is now correctly described and F36 says ESD only. That correction resolves
the former package error. However, the diode does not establish a clamp below the
regulators' input absolute maximum. Selecting it as the only input-voltage protection
would still need justification.

Recommended correction/check: Keep it if its ESD role is appropriate. If the BOM is
intended to include sustained-overvoltage or low-clamp protection, select a separate
suitable protection component against the actual waveform and ratings. Do not treat a
generic lower-VCL TVS suggestion as a verified replacement. [3,7]

------------------------------------------------------------------------

12. LDO dissipation and worst-case heat claim remain unproven
Status: MEDIUM / CONDITIONAL SELECTION RISK
Cells: BOM!D16,L16; Spec!B8,B10

At 5 V to 3.3 V and 0.2 A, TLV75533PDBVR dissipates about 0.34 W. TI lists DBV junction-
to-ambient values of 100.8 C/W on its EVM and 231.1 C/W under its stated JEDEC
conditions: illustrative rises of about 34 C and 79 C. Neither is a prediction for the
project. Spec B10 labels estimated converter loss worst case without a full operating
bound.

Recommended correction/check: Keep the regulator provisionally, but qualify maximum
load, input and ambient before approving it. Label the heat estimate as an estimate at
stated conditions unless worst-case losses are calculated. DYD improves thermal behavior
but adds an exposed-pad assembly requirement. [8]

------------------------------------------------------------------------

13. Ferrite DCR assumption is not its guaranteed maximum
Status: MEDIUM / CONDITIONAL SELECTION RISK
Cells: BOM!D25,F25,L25

BLM18PG300SN1 is listed by Murata at 1 A and 0.05 ohm maximum DCR. The BOM says
approximately 40 milliohm and requires less than 50 milliohm. A maximum of exactly 50
milliohm does not guarantee strictly less than 50 milliohm. At 0.7 A it gives 35 mV
drop; 1.096 V becomes approximately 1.061 V before other effects.

Recommended correction/check: Use maximum DCR and actual maximum current in selection
calculations. Correct the strict requirement or select a lower-DCR bead if it must be
met. This nominal voltage-drop calculation alone does not prove failure. [9]

------------------------------------------------------------------------

14. High-capacitance MLCC MPNs and effective capacitance need manufacturer verification
Status: MEDIUM / UNVERIFIED EXACT PARTS
Cells: BOM!D17,D18,D21,D22,D26,D42 and related Buy_List rows

Exact manufacturer records were not established for GRM188R70J106ME47D,
GRM188R70J226ME47D and GRM155R70J106ME15D. Failure to find a record is not proof that a
part is invalid. The selected small-case, high-capacitance parts also require DC-bias
evaluation; marked capacitance is not necessarily effective capacitance at operating
voltage.

Recommended correction/check: Verify each exact ordering code, dielectric, package,
voltage rating and bias curve with Murata before ordering. Use verified minimum
effective capacitance for the regulators. Do not silently replace a possibly mistyped
MPN with a similar code. A 6.3 V part on 5 V is not automatically wrong, but transient
margin needs consideration.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

15. Hand-solder suitability applies to converters, not automatically every part
Status: MEDIUM / CONDITIONAL ASSEMBLY RISK
Cells: BOM!G5,G8:G11,G34:G35; small passives and connectors

The selected converters are gull-wing, but QFN/USON devices and very small passives
remain. Spec B12 specifically describes the converters and should not be generalized to
iron-only assembly of the whole BOM.

Recommended correction/check: Confirm suitable reflow/hot-air capability or outsourced
assembly for inaccessible pads. Do not reject otherwise suitable parts merely because
the workbook name says Hand-Solder.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

16. Source budget, switch thresholds and input components must be assessed together
Status: MEDIUM / CONDITIONAL POWER SELECTION RISK
Cells: Spec!B6:B7; BOM!B12:B15,L29,B40:B41

Three 0.9 A loads total 13.5 W before hub consumption; a 7.5 W host cannot supply that.
Three 1 A loads already equal 15 W before losses. A 24.9 kohm ILIM setting gives about
1.036 A nominal and approximately 0.953-1.130 A limits including 1% resistor tolerance;
it is not a precise 1.000 A cap. The 180 uF bulk capacitor and optional single N-FET
also need a compatible input-power role.

Recommended correction/check: State an aggregate load/source assumption in the BOM
specification. Select switches and input capacitance against that assumption, including
inrush and reverse-current requirements. A bare N-FET described as gate from VBUS detect
is not a self-contained high-side reverse-blocking solution. These are compatibility
checks, not findings about circuit completeness. [1,2]
