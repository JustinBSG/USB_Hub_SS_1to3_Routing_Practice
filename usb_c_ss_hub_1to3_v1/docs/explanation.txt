USB-C SS Hub Case C — Why Certain Review Items Remain Unchanged
================================================================
Date: 2 November 2026
Workbook: USB-C-SS-Hub-Case-C-BOM-Hand-Solder-Corrected-R2.xlsx
Source review: problem-2.txt

Scope of this note
------------------
Round 2 corrected confirmed documentation, formula, and label errors
(DNP-column misuse, conflicting CURRENT_MODE text, hard-coded ESD
calculations, absolute-max wording, crystal arithmetic, standoff
description, ILIM label, Isat criterion, Q1 policy note).

The items below were intentionally left unchanged. They are conditional
selection risks, require measured prototype data, or are outside a
pure BOM-text update. Changing them without that data would risk
introducing new errors.

Unchanged items and reasons
---------------------------

11. PESD5V0S1UL remains ESD-only (not low-voltage OVP)
    Status: HIGH / CONDITIONAL SELECTION RISK
    Why unchanged:
    - Package and “ESD only” description are already correct.
    - VBR ≥ 6.4 V and high VCL do not guarantee clamping below the
      TLV62569 6 V absolute maximum.
    - Replacing it with a different TVS requires a verified clamping
      curve at the expected surge current, source impedance, and
      layout. A generic “lower-VCL” suggestion is not a qualified
      drop-in.
    Action still required:
    Keep for ESD if that is the only requirement. Add a separate OVP
    or eFuse if sustained over-voltage protection is a real need.
    Validate on the first prototype.

12. LDO thermal margin (TLV75533PDBVR at 0.34 W)
    Status: MEDIUM / CONDITIONAL
    Why unchanged:
    - 0.2 A / 1.7 V drop = 0.34 W is correct arithmetic.
    - TI θJA figures (100.8 °C/W EVM, 231.1 °C/W JEDEC) are
      illustrative only; they are not a prediction for this board’s
      copper, vias, ambient, or airflow.
    - Switching to the DYD (exposed-pad) package improves thermal
      performance but adds an assembly requirement that contradicts
      the pure-iron hand-solder goal for the converters.
    Action still required:
    Measure junction temperature (or case temperature + θJC) on the
    first board at maximum load and ambient. Retain DBV only if the
    result stays inside the safe operating area.

13. Ferrite bead DCR (BLM18PG300SN1D)
    Status: MEDIUM / CONDITIONAL
    Why unchanged:
    - Murata maximum DCR is 0.05 Ω. The BOM note “DCR < 50 mΩ” is
      therefore slightly stricter than the guaranteed maximum.
    - At 0.7 A the worst-case drop is 35 mV (1.096 V → ~1.061 V).
      This alone does not prove a functional failure for the 1.1 V
      rail.
    Action still required:
    Use maximum DCR and maximum rail current in the voltage-budget
    calculation. Replace only if the margin is inadequate after
    including regulator tolerance, transient droop, and layout IR drop.

14. High-capacitance MLCC exact MPNs and DC-bias effective capacitance
    Status: MEDIUM / UNVERIFIED EXACT PARTS
    Why unchanged:
    - Failure to locate an exact manufacturer record in a quick search
      is not proof that GRM188R70J106ME47D, GRM188R70J226ME47D, or
      GRM155R70J106ME15D are invalid.
    - Marked capacitance is not effective capacitance under DC bias.
      Bias curves must come from Murata for the exact ordering code.
    Action still required:
    Verify each MPN against the current Murata catalog and obtain the
    bias derating curve. Use the minimum effective capacitance in the
    regulator stability calculation. Do not silently substitute a
    similar-looking code.

15. Hand-solder suitability (QFN / USON / 0402)
    Status: MEDIUM / CONDITIONAL ASSEMBLY RISK
    Why unchanged:
    - The Spec sheet already limits the “hand-solder” claim to the two
      SOT-23 converters.
    - Rejecting otherwise suitable ICs solely because the workbook name
      contains “Hand-Solder” would force a larger redesign without
      solving the electrical issues.
    Action still required:
    Confirm hot-air, stencil/reflow, or outsourced assembly for the
    TUSB8041A, HD3SS3220, and small ESD arrays. Pure iron is not
    reliable for inaccessible thermal pads.

16. Source budget, switch thresholds, and input components
    Status: MEDIUM / CONDITIONAL POWER SELECTION RISK
    Why unchanged:
    - The BOM already states ~1 A per port and Default USB current
      advertisement. Three ports at that level can exceed a 7.5 W host.
    - The 180 µF bulk and optional N-FET reverse-block path are
      interdependent; changing one without the other can create new
      inrush or reverse-current problems.
    Action still required:
    State an aggregate load/source assumption in the Spec sheet.
    Validate inrush with the chosen bulk capacitor and confirm the
    reverse-blocking state of Q1 (or replace with an integrated
    solution) before production.

Summary
-------
The Round-2 workbook is now consistent on the items that could be
corrected from datasheet text and arithmetic alone. The remaining items
require either (a) measured prototype data or (b) a deliberate system-
level decision (OVP requirement, host power budget, assembly process).
Leaving them unchanged avoids introducing unverified substitutions
while keeping the open risks visible in the notes.
